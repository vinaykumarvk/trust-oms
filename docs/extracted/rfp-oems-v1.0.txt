	•	BACKGROUND 

1 LATAR BELAKANG

Wealth Management (WM) is one of the sectors that Danamon wants to focus on since 2023. Digitizing wealth management transaction process is crucial for Danamon to be able to compete with other banks, to serve customers better and have more efficient internal processes. A system is needed to change the manual process to digitizing and system-based process for wealth management products’ order processing. Products and digitizing process that Danamon wants to focus on and expected to be catered on the new system are foreign exchange (FX) transaction (especially for FX Leave Order and FX Transaction), Structured Product (MLD & DCI), Mutual Funds, and Bonds.



In terms of the channel, for all WM products mentioned above is expected to be available on online channel and branch. Customer can perform the transaction by their own (self-service) via Danamon online channel (D-Bank PRO), furthermore Sales will also be able to assist customer to perform the transaction using e-form via Order Management System (directly or via microsite-integrated with CRM – Customer Relationship Management). Moreover, to enhance the process of transactions, it is expected that customers can authorize the transaction via digital signature or digital verification.

With this increase in wealth management capabilities, it is hoped that Bank Danamon can:
	•	Increase customer's stickiness by offering an easy and convenient alternative to invest/transact in wealth management products.
	•	Provide several platforms that can let the customers do transactions for wealth management products at various channels including offline and online channels.
	•	Increase the volume transaction of wealth management products.
	•	Improve work effectiveness by transforming transaction processes towards system-based activities.
	•	Increase CASA balance
	•	Increase fee-based income
	•	Increase brand awareness
	•	Increase NTB
	•	Improve the investment transaction TAT (turnaround time) will benefit both banks and customers

	•	CURRENT SITUATION 
	•	SITUASI TERKINI
2.1 FX LEAVE ORDER (ODA)
	•	Offering and execution transactions for product FX Leave Order is currently performed manually. Order collection is handled manually by staff at branch. There is no integrated order management system to handle these transactions. 
	•	Customers must go to the branch or place order using Phone, Fax or Email (PFE) facility if they want to request for FX Leave Order (ODA) transaction. But order collection is still done manually by staff at branch.
	•	Order grouping and validation minimum amount (based on currency pair, rate, and orer type) can only be done after all orders received and manually checked.
	•	Order taking and order collection for transactions of product FX Leave Order, cannot be performed via CRM by Sales.
	•	Notifications to customer regarding the transaction result, whether it’s success or failed, are performed manually by phone or email by Sales.
	•	Currently, the FX ODA recommendation is only for information, sent via email/WA to the sales team. There is no system that can provide recommendation to sales based on parameter that has been set.

Manual process for FX Leave Order transactions:
	•	For new customer who wants to transact FX Leave Order, they need to sign paper-based SKU (general T&C) & Phone, Fax, or Email (PFE), and Indemnity document. Then, SSO (branch operation) to input/register the data manually on Core Banking System.
	•	Customers need to fill in Instruction Form (paper based), sales to ensure document completeness, and SSO (branch operation) to keep these documents as proof of instruction. If customer has a PFE facility, they may place order through PFE, however order collection is still done manually by staff at branch.
	•	BSM (Branch Sales Manager) to verify all documents (paper-based), validates the transaction, instruct SSO to block/hold customer balance, and send transaction details to PIC ODA (dedicated PIC for FX Leave Order in each region) via email.
	•	PIC ODA will check the order and input it on ODA Blotter (spreadsheet) then send it to Treasury team.
	•	Treasury team manually check the minimum collective order per currency pair and rate on the ODA Blotter. If it meets the minimum requirement, ODA order is placed, then it will be continued to observation period, else Treasury will inform PIC ODA and BSM regarding the update status of ODA order. 
	•	On every Expiry date, BSM will inform SSO to unblock customer balance hold (via Core Banking System. 
	•	On expiry date, Treasury will check manually the deals that due on that day, whether it is executed or expired. 
	•	If the order is expired, then Treasury will inform PIC ODA and BSM regarding the result. 
	•	If the order is executed, then Treasury will inform PIC ODA and BSM regarding the result. Treasury will upload the ODA transactions on Core Banking System, transaction will be recorded in Core Banking System and Treasury System.
	•	For executed order, if status transaction is not auto settled, teller needs to perform manual overbook in Core Banking System. BSM need to ensure overbook process is successful and status of transaction is already settled.
	•	BSM/Sales representative to inform the customer that status of ODA transaction has successfully executed or ODA transaction is expired, manually via phone/email.

	•	FX LEAVE ORDER (ODA)
	•	Penawaran dan transaksi produk FX Leave Order saat ini dilakukan secara manual. Pengambilan pesanan dilakukan secara manual oleh staf di cabang. Tidak ada Order Management Sistem terintegrasi untuk menangani transaksi ini.Nasabah harus datang ke cabang atau melakukan pemesanan melalui Phone, Fax atau Email Facility (PFE) jika ingin melakukan permintaan transaksi FX Leave Order (ODA). Namun pengambilan pesanan masih dilakukan secara manual oleh staf di cabang.Pengelompokan pesanan dan jumlah minimum (berdasarkan pasangan mata uang, kurs, dan jenis) hanya dapat dilakukan setelah semua pesanan masuk dan diperiksa secara manual.Order taking dan order collection untuk transaksi produk FX Leave Order, tidak dapat dilakukan melalui CRM oleh Sales.
	•	Pemberitahuan kepada nasabah atas hasil transaksi berhasil atau gagal dilakukan secara manual melalui telepon atau email oleh Sales.
	•	Saat ini, FX ODA rekomendasi hanya informasi yang dikirim melalui email/WA kepada tim sales. Belum ada sistem yang dapat memberikan rekomendasi kepada user berdasarkan parameter yang telah ditetapkan


Proses manual untuk transaksi FX Leave Order:
	•	Untuk nasabah baru yang ingin melakukan transaksi FX Leave Order, nasabah harus menandatangani Dokumen berbasis kertas yaitu SKU (S&K umum) & Dokumen PFE (Telepon, Faks, atau Email), dan dokumen Indemnity. Kemudian SSO (operasional cabang) memasukkan data tersebut secara manual di Sistem Core Banking. 
	•	Nasabah perlu mengisi Formulir Instruksi (berbasis kertas), Sales memastikan kelengkapan dokumen, SSO (operasional cabang) untuk menyimpan dokumen-dokumen ini sebagai bukti instruksi. Jika nasabah memiliki fasilitas PFE, mereka dapat memesan melalui PFE, namun pengumpulan pesanan masih dilakukan secara manual oleh staf di cabang.
	•	BSM (Branch Sales Manager) melakukan verifikasi atas semua dokumen (berbasis kertas), melakukan validasi transaksi, menginstruksikan SSO untuk memblokir/menahan saldo nasabah, dan mengirimkan detail transaksi ke 
	•	PIC ODA (PIC khusus untuk FX Leave Order di setiap wilayah) melalui surat elektronik.PIC ODA akan mengecek pesanan dan menginputnya di ODA Blotter (spreadsheet) kemudian mengirimkannya ke tim Treasury.
	•	Tim Treasury memeriksa pesanan kolektif minimum per pasangan mata uang dan kurs, secara manual di ODA Blotter. Jika memenuhi persyaratan minimum, pemesanan ODA dilakukan, kemudian akan dilanjutkan ke periode pengamatan, jika tidak, Treasury akan menginformasikan hasilnya kepada PIC ODA dan BSM.Pada setiap Expiry date, BSM akan menginformasikan kepada SSO untuk membuka blokir saldo yang ditahan nasabah (melalui Sistem Core Banking Treasury memeriksa secara manual transaksi yang jatuh tempo pada hari itu, apakah tereksekusi atau expired.
	•	Jika order expired, maka Treasury akan menginformasikan hasilnya kepada PIC ODA dan BSM. 
	•	Jika order tereksekusi, maka Treasury akan menginformasikan hasilnya kepada PIC ODA dan BSM. Treasury akan mengunggah transaksi ODA pada Sistem Core Banking, transaksi akan tercatat di Sistem Core System dan Sistem Treasury.Untuk order yang tereksekusi, jika status transaksi tidak auto settle, teller perlu melakukan pindah buku manual di Sistem Core Banking. BSM perlu memastikan proses pemindahbukuan berhasil dan transaksi sudah diselesaikan.
	•	BSM/Sales menginformasikan kepada nasabah bahwa transaksi ODA telah berhasil dilaksanakan atau transaksi ODA telah expired, secara manual melalui telepon/email.
 

2.2 STRUCTURED PRODUCTS – MLD 
	•	The offering of product, order collection and performing transactions for product Structured Products (MLD) are currently performed manually. There is no integrated order management system to handle these transactions.
	•	Customers must go to the branch to place orders or use Phone, Fax or Email Facility (PFE) if they want to request a contract for Structured Products (MLD) transaction.
	•	Order taking & order collection for transactions of product Structured Products (MLD), cannot be performed via CRM by Sales.
	•	The 90-day average balance (CASA and TD non-Structured Product/on balance sheet) for the same currency needs to be checked manually from different systems as a basis for minimum MLD booking eligibility.
	•	Notification to the customer for the transaction result, whether customer received minimum or maximum return, is performed manually by phone or email by Sales.


Manual process for Market Linked Deposit (MLD) transactions:
	•	PIC Treasury and Wealth Management update indicative term sheet and determine the interest rate and payout rate.
	•	Customers sign paper-based documents such as SKU (general T&C) & Phone, Fax, or Email (PFE), and Indemnity document. Then, SSO (branch operation) input/register the data manually on Core Banking System.
	•	Customers need to fill in the Participation Form (paper based), sales to copy customer ID and SSO (branch operation) to keep these documents as proof of instruction.
	•	Manual checking of minimum booking amount should be equal to or less than 90 days’ average balance for the currency chosen.
	•	SSO & BSM (Branch Sales Manager) to verify data & all documents (paper-based), update customer data & risk profile on CIM09 Core Banking System.
	•	SSO call back to customer to confirm on the placement then block/hold customer balance and send MLD recapitulation to WM team via email.
	•	Wealth Management team to compile MLD recapitulation to Treasury team after COT (cut off time) of offering period.
	•	On the trade date, WM team performs customer data matching and MLD recapitulation then send the MLD Final Blotter to TIWO & Treasury.
	•	On the trade date, PIC Treasury updates the final term sheet and deals with the counterparty. Then input option transaction in Treasury System.
	•	On the trade date, TIWO place Time Deposit. Then PIC TIWO send MLD participation confirmation letter via manual mailing (courier). PIC TIWO also performs reconciliation between Treasury System & Core Banking System.
	•	On the fixing date, if it’s on strike condition, PIC Treasury will exercise the transaction on Treasury System. Treasury will inform MLD result to WM, TIWO, and Sales via email. 
	•	PIC TIWO will send MLD participation result confirmation letter to customer via manual mailing (courier).
	•	On maturity date, TIWO credits the TD disbursement and minimum interest, deducted by tax. On Maturity date + 2, PIC TIWO also perform crediting of payout (if any) to customer’s account and pay premium to counterparty.

Proses manual untuk transaksi Market Linked Deposit (MLD):
	•	PIC Treasury and Wealth Management perlu memperbarui term sheet indikatif dan menentukan suku bunga dan pembayaran.
	•	Nasabah menandatangani dokumen berbasis kertas yaitu SKU (S&K umum) & Telepon, Faks, atau Email (PFE), dan dokumen Indemnity. Kemudian SSO/Sales (perwakilan cabang) memasukkan data tersebut secara manual di Core Banking System. 
	•	Nasabah harus mengisi Formulir Partisipasi (berbasis kertas), Sales menyalin ID nasabah dan SSO/Sales (perwakilan cabang) menyimpan dokumen-dokumen ini sebagai bukti instruksi.
	•	Pengecekan manual jumlah pemesanan minimum harus sama dengan atau kurang dari saldo rata-rata 90 hari untuk mata uang yang dipilih.
	•	SSO & BSM (Branch Sales Manager) melakukan verifikasi data & seluruh dokumen (paper-based), update data nasabah & risk profile pada CIM09 Sistem Core Banking.
	•	SSO callback ke nasabah untuk konfirmasi penempatan lalu blokir/tahan saldo nasabah dan kirimkan rekapitulasi MLD ke tim WM melalui email.
	•	Tim WM menyusun rekapitulasi MLD ke tim Treasury setelah COT (cut off time).
	•	Pada tanggal transaksi, tim WM melakukan pencocokan data nasabah dan rekapitulasi MLD kemudian mengirimkan Final Blotter MLD ke TIWO & Treasury.
	•	Pada tanggal transaksi, PIC Treasury memperbarui term sheet terakhir dan melakukan transaksi dengan counterparty. Kemudian input transaksi opsi di Sistem Treasury.
	•	Pada tanggal transaksi, TIWO menempatkan Deposito Berjangka. Kemudian PIC TIWO mengirimkan surat konfirmasi kepesertaan MLD melalui manual mailing (kurir). PIC TIWO juga melakukan rekonsiliasi antara Sistem Treasury & Sistem Core Banking.
	•	Pada tanggal fixing, jika teradi strike, PIC Treasury akan melaksanakan transaksi di Sistem Treasury. Treasury akan menginformasikan hasil MLD kepada WM, TIWO, dan Sales melalui email.
	•	PIC TIWO akan mengirimkan surat konfirmasi hasil partisipasi MLD kepada customer melalui manual mailing (kurir).
	•	Pada saat jatuh tempo, PIC TIWO mengkreditkan pencairan deposito dan bunga minimum dipotong pajak. Pada tanggal jatuh tempo + 2, PIC TIWO juga melakukan pengkreditan pembayaran (jika ada) ke rekening nasabah dan membayar premi ke counterparty.

4.2.2 FX LEAVE ORDER (ODA)
4.2.2.1 PRE-ORDER CHECK & REGISTRATION

	•	Implementing a new Order Management System that can capture order for FX Leave Order (ODA) product.
	•	Order capture/registration is done by filling e-form and can be performed by:
	•	Sales team via Order Management System.
	•	Sales team via CRM (microsite Order Management System).
	•	Customer via Danamon Internet Banking/Mobile Banking System (D-Bank PRO). 
	•	Upon submission, Order Management System needs to perform several validations such as: minimum order placement amount, customer balance check, CIF validation, currency pairing & rate, SKU, & Phone Indemnity.
	•	For order that submitted by sales, order needs to be checked & authorize by BSM on Order Management System (if customer digital signature/verification has not been implemented). Authorization by BSM by default should be on the same branch, but system should have flexibility to open for all branches. 
	•	Order Management System will support both individual and corporate customer.
	•	For FX Leave Order transaction, Order Management System can cater these types of transactions:
	•	ODA transaction based on type:
	•	Single order
	•	If Done Order
	•	One-Cancel-Other Order (OCO)
	•	ODA transaction based on effective date: 
	•	Intraday 
	•	Overnight
	•	Good ‘till (Date & Time)
	•	Order Management System to interface with Treasury System to retrieve reference rate data on daily basis or ad hoc per request.
	•	Order Management System to display and send trade ideas / ODA recommendation to customer. When customer interested on this trade ideas, customer will be directed to the e-form with the autofill rate.
	•	Order Management System can show daily order summary per currency pairing and per rate, for Sales to check how close it is to the minimum collective order.
	•	For Order submission there is cut-off time (parameterized), so for order that is inputted after cut-off time, it cannot be submitted.



4.2.2.2 ORDER COLLECTION & ORDER PLACEMENT
	•	Once order is submitted and authorized, Order Management System instructs Core Banking System to block/hold customer balance (as per transaction amount).
	•	Order Management System then record the transaction on the FX ODA Blotter and user can see this Blotter on the screen of Order Management System.
	•	At cut off time on daily basis (can be different based on ODA transaction type), Order Management System will perform checking of the total amount of order by:
	•	Direction
	•	Currency pairs
	•	Rate
	•	Order Cost (Before calculated with swap points)
whether it meets the minimum collective order or not. 
	•	If meets the minimum collective order, order will be put to Summary Blotter which later will be placed on Treasury Trading System, manually by Treasury traders.
	•	If doesn’t meet the minimum collective order, order will be recorded by Order Management System, but the status is cancelled and not included in Summary Blotter. Order Management System needs to instruct Core Banking System to unhold/unblock customer balance. Order Management System also inform BSM/Sales and customer regarding the cancellation of order via email.
	•	At the end of day (or at time agreed), Order Management System needs to generate ODA Blotter for all transaction of that day (before COT that day) and Treasury team can download this summary ODA Blotter from Order Management System.
	•	  System should have amendment/ modification process/function. Order amendment can be done if checker rejects transaction submitted by maker and need modification by maker. Amendment must be done before transaction COT.
	•	 System should have cancellation process/function. Order cancellation is same as deleting an order. Cancellation must be done before daily COT.




4.2.2.3 ORDER OBSERVATION & EXECUTION
	•	Treasury traders will update manually the status and Swap points on ODA summary order on Order Management System on daily basis, whether it’s executed or expired or still in observation period.
	•	If transaction is executed, 
	•	Order Management System instructs Core Banking System to unhold/unblock customer balance
	•	Order Management System instructs Core Banking System to overbook customer’s FX Leave Order transaction and retrieve the result whether the transaction is auto-settle or not. If transaction is not auto-settle, then Order Management System send notification to BSM/SSO to perform manual overbook. 
	•	Core Banking System instructs Treasury System to book the transaction following current FP 8007 – NCBS Menu.
	•	Order Management System also inform BSM/Sales and customer via email that the order is executed.
	•	Status that is being updated in ODA blotter in Order Management System will be update to FP 8007 (NCBS Menu).
	•	If transaction is expired, Order Management System needs to instruct Core Banking System to unhold/unblock customer balance. Order Management System also inform BSM/Sales and customer regarding the expiration of order via email.
	•	If order still in observation period, system will still keep and manage the order until executed or expired.
	•	User can see the status of ODA transactions on Order Management System.
	•	There is maker-checker process upon Treasury traders update the status of ODA summary order on Order Management System.









4.2.2.4 CALCULATION

	•	Order Management System should be able to calculate nominal order for ODA transaction, as in the total amount should be overbooked for the transaction, calculated based on the order amount and hit rate.
	•	Order Management System should be able to calculate and capture the nominal order when transaction is submitted, executed, or expired.

4.2.3 STRUCTURE PRODUCTS – MLD 
4.2.3.1 ORDER PROCESS 
4.2.3.1.1 OFFERING PERIOD 
	•	Market Linked Deposit (MLD) is one of derivative products in a form of Structured Product. It is a combination of 2 financial instruments: deposit and currency options offering a potential higher growth than traditional term deposit. The principal deposit is guaranteed if hold until maturity, and return is determined by market performance of the FX as the underlying financial instruments.
	•	Treasury/WM team to create MLD product on Order Management System.
	•	Sales offer MLD product (during offering period) to customer while showing indicative termsheet on Order Management System. If customer interested to buy, Sales create order / order collection on Order Management System. Sales to input customer CIF and Order Management System shall check to Core Banking System if CIF is valid. Core Banking System to give response of CIF details to Order Management System.
	•	Order Management System display customer detail page, which includes customer static data info and mandatory document signature info (SKU & PFE). If mandatory documents have not been signed by customer, customer need to sign mandatory documents (sign digitally on digital based documents): SKU, PFE, termsheet, product highlight sheet, risk profile questionnaire. Else, Sales continue the process by filling all the required details on order entry page.
	•	Order Management System displays MLD participation form (in digital form) and customer need to digitally sign or verify.
	•	Sales submit MLD order and upon submission, Order Management System needs to:
	•	Check MLD transaction amount is above minimum amount in indicative term sheet and maximum is same or below customer 90 days customer average balance. 
	•	Check if customer balance is available. If customer balance is not sufficient, customer need to top up the balance and MLD order process cannot be continued. If customer balance is sufficient, Order Management System to block/hold customer balance.
	•	Include order detail in daily order recap and blotter compilation.
	•	Check whether the amount and currency submitted equal to or less than 90 days average balance for the same currency.
	•	Order Management System displays that MLD order request has been successfully submitted. MLD order is available in final master blotter.
	•	Before trade date, Order Management System should be able to recheck all customers of that MLD Tranche for their 90 days average balance. Bank ensure that before trade date, customers are still maintaining their average balance.
	•	System should have amendment/modification process/function. Order amendment can be done if checker rejects transaction submitted by maker and need modification by maker. Admendment must be done before transaction COT.
	•	System should have cancellation process/function. Order cancellation is the same as deleting an order. Cancellation must be done before transaction COT.

	•	Market Linked Deposit (MLD) adalah salah satu produk derivatif berupa Structured Product. Ini adalah kombinasi dari 2 instrumen keuangan: deposito dan transaksi opsi mata uang, yang menawarkan potensi pertumbuhan yang lebih tinggi daripada deposito berjangka tradisional. Setoran pokok dijamin jika disimpan hingga jatuh tempo, dan pengembalian ditentukan oleh kinerja pasar FX sebagai instrumen keuangan yang mendasarinya.
	•	Tim Treasury/WM dapat membuat produk MLD pada Order Management System.

Jika nasabah tertarik untuk membeli, Sales membuat order / order collection di Order Management System. Sales memasukkan CIF nasabah, kemudian Order Management System melakukan pengecekan ke Sistem Core Banking apakah CIF valid. Sistem Core Banking memberikan respon detail CIF ke Order Management System.
	•	Order Management System menampilkan halaman detail nasabah, yang meliputi info data statis nasabah dan info tanda tangan dokumen wajib (SKU & PFE). Jika dokumen wajib belum ditandatangani oleh nasabah, nasabah harus menandatangani dokumen wajib (menandatangani secara digital pada dokumen berbasis digital): SKU, PFE, termsheet, product highlight sheet, kuesioner profil risiko. Selain itu, Sales melanjutkan proses dengan mengisi semua detail yang diperlukan di halaman entri pesanan.
	•	Order Management System menampilkan formulir partisipasi MLD (dalam bentuk digital) dan nasabah perlu menandatangani atau memverifikasi secara digital.bSales mengirimkan pesanan MLD dan setelah pengiriman, Order Management System perlu:bMemeriksa jumlah transaksi MLD di atas jumlah minimum dalam termsheet indikatif dan maksimum sama dengan atau di bawah saldo rata-rata 90 hari nasabah.bPeriksa apakah saldo nasabah tersedia. Jika saldo nasabah tidak mencukupi, nasabah harus melakukan top up saldo dan proses pemesanan MLD tidak dapat dilanjutkan. Jika saldo nasabah mencukupi, Order Management System memblokir/menahan saldo nasabah.bSertakan detail pesanan dalam rekap pesanan harian dan kompilasi blotter.bPeriksa apakah jumlah dan mata uang yang diajukan sama dengan atau kurang dari saldo rata-rata 90 hari untuk mata uang yang sama.bOrder Management System menampilkan bahwa permintaan pesanan MLD telah berhasil diajukan. Pesanan MLD tersedia di final master blotter.
	•	Sebelum tanggal transaksi, Order Management System harus dapat memeriksa kembali semua nasabah dari Tranche MLD tersebut untuk mengetahui saldo rata-rata 90 hari. Bank memastikan bahwa sebelum tanggal transaksi, nasabah masih mempertahankan saldo rata-rata mereka.

	•	Sistem harus memiliki proses/fungsi pembatalan order. Pembatalan order sama dengan penghapusan order. Pembatalan hanya dapat dilakukan sebelum transaksi COT.
	•	Sistem harus memiliki proses/fungsi untuk melakukan amendment atau modifikasi. Amendment pesanan dapat dilakukan apabila checker menolak transaksi yang diajukan oleh maker dan membutuhkan perbaikan oleh maker. Proses amendment harus dilakukan sebelum batas waktu Cut-Off Time (COT) transaksi
	•	Sistem harus memiliki proses/fungsi pembatalan. Pembatalan pesanan sama dengan menghapus pesanan. Proses pembatalan harus dilakukan sebelum batas waktu Cut-Off Time (COT) transaksi.

	•	Pada fase satu, proses pendaftaran terbatas pada mendaftarkan fasilitas pinjaman yang telah dibuat secara manual. User dapat menginput informasi dari pinjaman dan juga collateral yang digunakan 




4.2.6.2 MARK TO MARKET
	•	Order Management System able to generate mark to market for each customer by: 
	•	Retrieve daily market price data for Bonds and Mutual Funds, from Wealth Core System (RBS and Avatrade) 
	•	Retrieve data credit outstanding Loan Facility information amount from Loan System or Core Banking System  
	•	Order Management System able to perform calculation of the mark to market process (compare between the asset market price and overdraft/credit used by customer), Collateral value compare to loan limit and and determine if the credit limit is breached or not. 
	•	Order Management System to update the limit amount based on mark to market conditions. The limit will be update if collateral value is below Loan limit. This data should be passed to Loan System or Core Banking System as well by instruction (direct) or by generate report. 
	•	Customer able to see overdraft limit Loan Facility information on Internet Banking web/apps (D-Bank PRO). 
	•	Sales able to see customer’s overdraft limit loan facility information on Order Management System or CRM (Order Management System’s microsite). 
	•	Order Management System send instruction to Loan System or Core Banking System or generate report to block overdraft/credit facility if the overdraft limit is breached based on the mark to market process. 
	•	In the case where customer’s overdraft/credit is exceeding the limit based on the mark to market process, customer will be asked to repay the loan or increase the collateral to unblock the overdraft loan facility, for certain period. The period can be different for each decreasing percentage of collateral value. Order Management System should be able to calculate the amount of credit customer has to repay or amount of collateral that customer need to top up. 
	•	If customer choose to repay the loan, Order Management System to follow existing loan repayment process. Once repayment is done, Order Management System to re-calculate the limit and open the overdraft loan facility. 
	•	If customer choose to increase the collateral, Order Management System to bind new collateral, re-calculate the limit and open the overdraft loan facility. 
	•	If until the end of period, customer has not repay the loan or increase the collateral (default condition), Order Management System will instruct Wealth Core System (RBS) to sell the collateral. 


4.2.6.2 Mark to Market
	•	Order Management System mampu menghasilkan mark to market untuk setiap nasabah dengan cara:
	•	Mengambil data harga pasar harian untuk Obligasi dan Reksa Dana dari Wealth Core System (RBS dan Avantrade).
	•	Mengambil data credit outstanding informasi fasilitas pinjaman dari Loan System atau Core Banking System.
	•	Order Management System mampu melakukan perhitungan proses mark to market (membandingkan antara harga pasar aset dengan overdraft/kredit yang digunakan oleh nasabah), membandingkan nilai agunan dengan batas pinjaman, dan menentukan apakah batas kredit terlampaui atau tidak.
	•	Order Management System akan memperbarui jumlah limit berdasarkan kondisi mark to market. Limit akan diperbarui apabila nilai agunan berada di bawah batas pinjaman. Data ini harus dikirimkan ke Loan System atau Core Banking System, baik melalui instruksi langsung maupun melalui laporan yang dihasilkan.
	•	Nasabah dapat melihat informasi limit overdraft fasilitas pinjaman melalui Internet Banking web/apps (D-Bank PRO).
	•	Sales dapat melihat informasi limit overdraft fasilitas pinjaman nasabah pada Order Management System atau CRM (microsite Order Management System).
	•	Order Management System mengirimkan instruksi ke Loan System atau Core Banking System atau menghasilkan laporan untuk memblokir fasilitas overdraft/kredit apabila limit overdraft terlampaui berdasarkan hasil proses mark to market.
	•	Dalam kasus ketika overdraft/kredit nasabah melebihi limit berdasarkan proses mark to market, nasabah akan diminta untuk melakukan pelunasan pinjaman atau menambah agunan untuk membuka kembali fasilitas overdraft, dalam jangka waktu tertentu. Periode waktu dapat berbeda tergantung pada persentase penurunan nilai agunan. Order Management System harus mampu menghitung jumlah kredit yang harus dilunasi nasabah atau nilai agunan yang perlu ditambah.
	•	Apabila nasabah memilih untuk melunasi pinjaman, Order Management System mengikuti proses pelunasan pinjaman yang berlaku. Setelah pelunasan selesai, sistem akan menghitung ulang limit dan membuka fasilitas overdraft.
	•	Apabila nasabah memilih untuk menambah agunan, Order Management System akan mengikat agunan baru, menghitung ulang limit, dan membuka kembali fasilitas overdraft.
	•	Apabila hingga akhir periode nasabah tidak melunasi pinjaman atau tidak menambah agunan (kondisi default), Order Management System akan menginstruksikan Wealth Core System (RBS) untuk menjual agunan tersebut.
