OMS Improvement BRD Council Report

Date: 2026-05-06

Subject: Danamon OEMS modernization BRD

Chairman Verdict

Revise and approve the BRD as a controlled banking change program, not as a broad feature backlog. The first executable proof must be ODA-first and must prove the minimum trustworthy order spine: active product/security reference, complete capture, source evidence, deterministic validation, workflow queue, audit event, and outbox event.

Minimum Trustworthy Order ODA First Policy-To-Rule Traceability Migration Runbook Operating Model

Where The Council Agreed

  • The target capabilities are directionally right but too broad for one execution wave.
  • ODA should prove the shared spine before broader product migration.
  • Rule governance and source evidence are launch gates, not polish.
  • Control tower analytics should follow reliable event and audit data.
